honest limits

What Stamp
doesn't claim.

  1. Binance's wallet and Trading API don't know about Stamp tickets. A caller that skips Stamp can still trade - the guarantee covers orders that go through it.
  2. bStocks report no market session and no stock price. Stamp uses the venue session and a sibling's price or its own recorded close, and says so on the ticket.
  3. xStock data disagrees with itself: one endpoint said NFLXx holds 10 shares, the other said 1. Stamp blocks that instead of choosing.
  4. The recorded close is the print at Binance's 20:00 session pause, taken every ten minutes - close to the official close, not the auction tape.
  5. Buy only, spot only, BSC only, $20 an order, and a person signs every fill.
