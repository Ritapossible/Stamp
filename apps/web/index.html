how it works

Say it plainly.
Code decides. You sign.

  1. You say the order. "Buy $20 of NVIDIA", the way you'd say it. A fixed grammar reads it - no model guesses what you meant.
  2. Stamp runs the checks. Live Binance data, one issuer, the real share count, halts, the off-hours premium. ALLOW, WARN or BLOCK, with a hash anyone can recompute.
  3. You sign in Binance Wallet. Only an ALLOW gets a quote. The quote and the transaction are checked against the ticket, then your wallet asks you. Stamp never holds a key.
