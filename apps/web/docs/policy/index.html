Policy

Ondo only. Never switch issuer. Never guess what "1" means. Don't buy while a stock is halted. Don't pay more than 0.30% over the stock while the US market is open, or 0.80% over the last close while it's shut. At most $20 per order, and $50 a day for standing orders. A person signs every order.

The same values live in packages/engine/src/policy.ts. Their hash, policyHash, is on every ticket, so a ticket made under a different policy can't pass for one made under this one.

Overriding it per request

Send a partial policy with POST /v1/tickets. Unknown keys are rejected and the result is validated again, so an override can tighten or loosen numbers but never turn off neverSwitchIssuer or the corporate-action block.

{ "intent": "Buy $20 of NVDAx", "policy": { "issuer": "xstock", "maxClosedPremiumBps": 50 } }

A WARN never leads to a signature. A standing order stays parked until a later check says ALLOW.
